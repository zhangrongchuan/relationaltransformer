\section{Implementation Details}
\label{app:details}

\paragraph{Context sampling.}
We use the sampler of \RT{} \citep[Section~3.1 and Appendix~A]{ranjan2026rt}.
Starting from the query row, it first follows child-to-parent references and
then samples pending children from the shallowest depth. A child is eligible
only if it precedes $t_g$ when both timestamps are known. At most 256
children from database tables are sampled per expansion, and task-table
children must belong to the current task. Designated leakage fields are
removed, and the context is capped at 2{,}048 cells.

\paragraph{Cell and time encoding.}
Column descriptions and text values are embedded by a frozen 384-dimensional
sentence encoder, as in \RT{}. Numeric and Boolean values are standardized per
column, and datetimes globally. The time encoding $\psi$ applies a linear map
to $\big[\cos(2\pi\Delta_r/\lambda_i),\sin(2\pi\Delta_r/\lambda_i)\big]_{i=1}^{16}$,
with wavelengths $\lambda_i$ spaced logarithmically from one day to about
100 years.

\paragraph{Numeric targets.}
Numeric targets occur only in source training. For them, the two class nodes
are replaced by 33 value nodes placed at the standard-normal quantiles of 33
evenly spaced levels from 0.005 to 0.995, and initialized with the numeric
value encoder plus $\mathbf e_{\mathrm{class}}$. Each earlier task row sends
label edges to the two value nodes that bracket its clipped label, with
linear-interpolation weights that also weight the mean in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:rowmp}. The scorer of Eq.~\eqref{eq:readout} gives one score per
value node, and the softmax over these scores is trained with cross-entropy
against the interpolated two-hot target. Losses are summed over targets and
divided by the number of targets in the batch.

\paragraph{Parameters and cost.}
With $d=256$, \model{} has 2.78M parameters, of which RelationGraph and the
projection $W_{\mathrm{cond}}$ account for 0.72M. The cost of each RelationGraph
layer is quadratic in the number of relations in the context, which is
bounded by the number of table pairs in the schema.
